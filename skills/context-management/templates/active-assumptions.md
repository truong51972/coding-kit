# Active Assumptions

Store **accepted long-lived operating assumptions** that future sessions must
preserve, clearly labelled as assumptions until factually verified. Provisional
task hypotheses belong in a task/handoff artifact or an explicitly designated
project ledger, not here. See `references/assumptions.md` in the skill.

## Assumptions

- Add assumptions only when they affect future work, have an authorized owner,
  and include scope and a condition for revalidation. Repetition is not proof.
- Do not rename an accepted assumption as a verified fact without supporting
  source or tests; put such facts in their owning context shard.

## Constraints

- Add durable constraints, defaults, or operating limits here when they affect future work.

## Review Policy

- Review assumptions when relevant owning source or policy changes. Retire
  superseded statements with a pointer to the successor when important.

## Exclusions

- Do not record decision history.
- Do not keep stale assumptions after source files or user direction supersede them.
